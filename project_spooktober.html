A short horror game about a young girl tying up lose ends

This game was made as part of the Spillklimpring "Spooktober Nights" game jam 2025. It was made in a single week, by me and a team of friends. The theme of the jam was "Don't look".

We wanted to create an experience inspired by indie horror RPGs of the 2010s, such as Ib, Ao Oni and Yume nikki. The game is a short top-down adventure with a few special mechanics to match the theme of the gamejam.

The main mechanic of the game is the player flashlight - the player has to use it to navigate the dark world, but looking at certain enemies will cause problems in turn. Aside from this, we wanted to fill the world with flavortext and fun events to make it feel like wandering around a haunted house.

My Contributions

In this project, I was the most experienced using Godot. As such I took on a lot of the underlying mechanics of the game, such as player interaction, inventory systems, scene switching, transitions and menus. Luckily the team learned super fast, and we were able to implement more features than I ever would have imagined in a week!

As for this project, I had the following responsibilities:

  • Designed and implemented the menus in the game: Title screen, pause menus, inventory, and display messages.
  • Designed a basic slot-based inventory system
  • Implemented a modular system for player/object interaction
  • Player Animation and other background assets
  • Iterated on gameplay ideas and story in collaboration with the team
